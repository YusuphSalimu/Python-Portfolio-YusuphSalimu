# Favicon Setup for Project Pages

## **Recommended Favicons:**

### **Project 1 - E-commerce Price Tracker:**
- **Theme:** Shopping/Price tracking
- **Color:** Blue/Green (money/finance)
- **Icon:** Shopping cart, price tag, or chart

### **Project 2 - Tanzania Stock Prediction:**
- **Theme:** Finance/Stock market
- **Color:** Red/Green (stock market colors)
- **Icon:** Stock chart, trend line, or finance graph

## **Sources for Free Favicons:**
- **Flaticon:** Free icons with attribution
- **Icons8:** Free basic icons
- **Font Awesome:** Use icon fonts
- **Pexels:** Free images (convert to favicon)
- **Pixabay:** Free vector icons

## **Implementation:**
1. Download 32x32 PNG icons
2. Save to assets/favicon/ folder
3. Update HTML <head> sections
4. Test in browser tabs
